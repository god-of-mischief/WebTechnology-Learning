To My Love, On Your 28th Birthday ❤️🌹 Happiest Birthday, my Baby Bangaram! ❤️🎂 Finally, the day I've been waiting for is here. I've been counting down the hours, minutes and seconds just to be among the first people to wish you. And at midnight I wished, but, I want to tell you something more than just Happy Birthday. Twenty-eight years ago, someone very special came into this world. Little did I know that one day, that person would become someone I'd care about so deeply, someone I'd miss, someone I'd worry about, someone I'd want to share my happiness with, and someone I'd start imagining my future around. Sometimes I wonder how life brings two people together. We didn't know what we would become to each other when we first started talking. We couldn't have predicted all the conversations, laughter, emotions, little arguments, surprises and memories that would follow. But somewhere along the way, you became an important part of my world. And I want you to know that I cherish that. I still remember the day we met. I remember the things you brought for me. That wallet with my name on it with B included 😅, the little crochet keychain, the beautiful crochet rose and, most importantly, the note. You might have thought they were just little gifts, but they meant so much more to me. Someone had thought about me, spent time choosing for me, and wanted to give me something I could keep. I still remember what the note said: "I don't need a perfect life, I just want a beautiful life with you." Bhavana, I don't know whether you understand how much those words mean to me. I kept thinking about them. And if there's one thing I want you to know today, it's that I want that beautiful life too. Not a life without problems. Not a life where we never disagree. Not a life where everything goes according to our plans. I want a life where we understand each other even when things get difficult. Where we can talk openly instead of keeping things inside. Where we can disagree without hurting each other. Where we celebrate each other's achievements and become each other's support during difficult times. I want the ordinary moments with you just as much as the extraordinary ones. I want to ask you how your day went. I want to listen to your endless stories, know about the little things that made you happy, laugh at your silly jokes, annoy you sometimes and make you smile when you least expect it. I want to be there for your victories, your disappointments, your dreams, your fears and all those moments when you don't even know how to explain what you're feeling. I want you to feel comfortable being yourself around me. You don't have to pretend to be strong every day. You don't have to hide your sadness. You don't have to be perfect. You can be silly, emotional, stubborn, quiet, talkative or anything in between. I want to know all those versions of you. And please, never stop dreaming about your own life. I want to see you achieve the things you've always wanted. I want to see you grow, explore, succeed and become the person you dream of becoming. Your happiness, individuality, ambitions and peace matter to me. I know our journey isn't going to be effortless. We have circumstances to deal with, differences to understand and challenges that we cannot simply wish away. There may be days when things feel difficult. But I hope we never forget why we started choosing each other. I don't want us to make promises that sound beautiful only in the moment. I want us to build something real through our actions, patience, honesty and understanding. I want us to become better people together. And my baby, there's something else I wish for you. I hope life becomes gentler with you. I hope you have more reasons to smile than to cry. I hope your mind finds peace, your heart feels safe, your efforts bring success and your dreams slowly become reality. Whenever you doubt yourself, I hope you remember how valuable you are. Never think that you aren't enough. Never underestimate yourself. You deserve happiness, respect, affection and a life in which you can be completely yourself. And now, coming to the birthday girl herself… 😂❤️ TWENTY-EIGHT! Another year older! Aunty, But please, never lose that playful, childish side of yours. Keep laughing at silly things, keep getting excited about little surprises, keep being curious about life and keep being the Bhavana I know. I wish I could go back in time, meet the little Bhavana who was born 28 years ago, give her a hug and tell her that someday she'll grow up to mean so much to someone. And if I could make one birthday wish on your behalf, it would be that every coming year gives you something beautiful to remember. As for my own wish, it's quite simple. I hope I get to celebrate many more birthdays with you. I hope someday we look back at this birthday, remember these messages, laugh about how I kept counting down the minutes and smile at how far we've come. I hope there are future birthdays with cakes, candles, photographs, surprises, silly arguments about what to eat, little trips, unexpected gifts and countless memories. I hope I get to stand beside you, hold your hand and wish you in person, not just through a screen. I don't know every detail of what tomorrow holds for us, but I know that I genuinely want to build a future with you. Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for the affection you've shown me. Thank you for the memories we've created. Thank you for the little things you do that perhaps you don't even realise I notice. And thank you for being you. Happy 28th Birthday, my Bujji dhana! ❤️ May this new chapter of your life be filled with beautiful beginnings, peaceful days, wonderful achievements and endless happiness. Here's to 28 years of YOU, and hopefully a lifetime of US. I don't need a perfect life, Bhavana. I just want that beautiful life with you. I love you. ❤️

This little website is just one small way of telling you that you deserve something beautiful.

With all my love,
Yours ❤️
