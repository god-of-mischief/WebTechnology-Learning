Why The BigBang Club ?

The Bigbang Club is one of those "It helped me alot in alot of ways" resources. Not everyone has access to the best computer science education or the funds to attend an intensive coding school and neither of those is right for everyone anyway. This project is designed to fill in the gap for people who are trying to hack it on their own but still want a high quality education.

Truth

Education should be accessible

Each and every one should study. education should be accessible to everyone. So every course on this site is accessible.

Education should be free

These courses themselves are free and we tried to link to resources that are themselves free so anyone in the world can use them.
